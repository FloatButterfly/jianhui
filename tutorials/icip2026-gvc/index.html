Tutorial outline

  1. Foundations of Generative Visual Coding — Giuseppe Valenzise
    Coding paradigms, rate–distortion–perception, diffusion priors, and posterior reconstruction.
  2. Diffusion Methods for Image and Video Coding — Jianhui Chang
    Bitstream representations, generation control, efficient decoding, and temporal modeling.
  3. Generative Face Video Coding and Beyond — Shiqi Wang
    Compact facial representations, motion modeling, scalable coding, standardization, and future directions.
